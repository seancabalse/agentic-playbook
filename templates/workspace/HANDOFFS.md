# Handoff log

The lead keeps this file. It contains each handoff between sessions, as it came, and the destination of each handoff. **Put new entries at the top.** Do not change an old entry. The history is important.

Each entry is a handoff with the shape of the playbook template `templates/handoff.md`. After the handoff, add this line:

**Routed**: <YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM UTC> to <session or owner>: <the work that they must do> · ticket comment: <link, or "not posted" and the reason>
